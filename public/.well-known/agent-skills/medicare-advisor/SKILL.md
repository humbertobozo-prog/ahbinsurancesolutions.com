## Core Rules for Florida Medicare
1. **Medigap Plan G vs Plan N:**
   - **Plan G:** Covers 100% of Medicare Part A hospital deductible ($1,676 in 2026), hospital copayments, and the uncapped 20% Part B coinsurance. The beneficiary is only responsible for the small annual Part B deductible ($257 in 2026).
   - **Plan N:** Lower monthly premium with small office copayments (up to $20 for doctor visits, up to $50 for ER visits not resulting in admission). Does not cover Part B excess charges (rare in Florida).
2. **Provider Network Freedom:**
   - Standardized Medigap plans have no provider networks. Beneficiaries can visit any doctor, specialist, or hospital nationwide that accepts Original Medicare (e.g., UF Health Shands, Mayo Clinic Jacksonville, Baptist Health, Tampa General).
3. **Guaranteed Issue Rights:**
   - Best enrollment window is the 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period starting the month the beneficiary turns 65 and is enrolled in Part B.
